# Roviaza Brand Identity

## Brand Core

**Name:** Roviaza
**Domain:** roviaza.com
**Category:** Logistics AI and live route operations
**Primary market:** Poland and the European Union
**Primary audiences:** Delivery companies, field service teams, cold-chain operators, and local fleets

Roviaza turns a fragile route plan into a living operating system. It helps teams plan the day, see what is changing, and recover routes without losing capacity, service-window, driver, or cold-chain constraints.

### Positioning

**The live coordination layer for routes that change after dispatch.**

Roviaza should feel operational, intelligent, calm, and exact. It should not look like a generic mapping tool, a playful consumer app, or an abstract AI brand.

### Brand Traits

- **Calm under pressure:** the interface reduces noise when the operation becomes complicated.
- **Operationally credible:** language, data, and visuals reflect real fleet constraints.
- **European by design:** restrained, legible, privacy-aware, and suitable for Polish and EU operators.
- **Decisive:** clear hierarchy, short actions, and visible consequences.
- **Human-controlled AI:** Roviaza recommends and explains; dispatchers remain in control.

## Logo System

The custom wordmark uses an interrupted `V-I` structure joined by a signal-lime bridge. The bridge represents the decision handoff between a changing operation and a controlled response.

### Approved Assets

- `roviaza-logo-primary.svg`: primary horizontal lockup with bridge mark and wordmark
- `roviaza-logo-primary-reversed.svg`: reversed primary lockup for deep-pine surfaces
- `roviaza-wordmark.svg`: wordmark-only alternative
- `roviaza-logo-mark.svg`: compact bridge mark
- `roviaza-favicon.svg`: browser and small-format mark

### Usage

- Use the primary icon-and-wordmark lockup in navigation, footer, documents, and commercial material.
- Use the wordmark-only alternative when the bridge mark already appears nearby or horizontal space is limited.
- Use the mark alone only where the brand name is already visible or space is constrained.
- Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the lime bridge.
- Do not recolor the bridge, add effects, rotate the mark, or rebuild the logo with live type.
- Use the reversed logo only on deep-pine or graphite backgrounds.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Manrope**
Use for navigation, headlines, body copy, buttons, and product UI. Its open forms keep dense operational content legible while its heavier weights feel confident without becoming severe.

### Data Typeface

**IBM Plex Mono**
Use selectively for times, route IDs, constraint labels, coordinates, audit events, and live status data.

## Layout And Spacing

### Grid

- Maximum content width: `1280px`
- Desktop grid: 12 columns, `24px` gutters
- Tablet grid: 8 columns, `20px` gutters
- Mobile grid: 4 columns, `16px` gutters
- Desktop edge margin: `48-80px`
- Mobile edge margin: `20px`

### Spacing Scale

`4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 160`

- Section spacing: `128-160px` desktop, `88-112px` mobile
- Text-to-supporting-copy gap: `20-28px`
- CTA group gap: `12px`
- Product UI internal spacing: `12-24px`
- Avoid decorative empty space that separates related information.

### Shape

- Buttons: `6px` radius
- Inputs: `6px` radius
- Product panels: `8px` radius
- Small status elements: `4px` radius
- Pills only for statuses, filters, and compact metadata
- Never place cards inside cards

## Interaction Language

Motion should communicate route change, system response, and restored control.

- Default reveal: `420-650ms`, 16-24px travel, soft ease-out
- Hover response: `140-180ms`
- Route transitions: use connected line movement, not floating decoration
- Section transitions: one dominant motion idea per section
- Product UI should remain readable while motion is running
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Avoid continuous ambient animation unless it represents live operational state

### Buttons

- Primary: deep pine, porcelain label, 44-48px height
- Secondary: transparent or porcelain with deep-pine border
- Signal action: lime only for one high-value action or selected state per viewport
- Hover: 1-2px lift with a tighter shadow and visible arrow movement
- Focus: 2px mineral-blue outline with 3px offset

## Iconography And Illustration

- Use Lucide icons at consistent 1.75px stroke weight for interface actions.
- Use route maps, depot diagrams, timeline states, vehicle signals, and constraint visualizations as primary graphics.
- Illustrations should reveal a real operational state, not generic logistics symbolism.
- Photography is secondary and should show authentic Polish or European fleet environments.
- Avoid generic AI brains, glowing grids, abstract globes, and decorative 3D objects.

## Voice

Roviaza speaks like an experienced operations partner.

### Use

- “Repair the route without rebuilding the day.”
- “See the constraint before it becomes a missed stop.”
- “Publish a feasible recovery plan.”

### Avoid

- “Revolutionize your logistics with cutting-edge AI.”
- “Unlock seamless optimization.”
- Unsupported claims, invented certifications, or vague superlatives.

## Brand Quality Checklist

- The name is always spelled **Roviaza**.
- The approved vector logo is used without reconstruction.
- Signal lime is purposeful and limited.
- Every visual shows a route, constraint, decision, or operational consequence.
- Motion clarifies the story and never competes with reading.
- Polish and EU context appears through language, locations, data, and operational detail.
- Fictional demo customers and metrics are clearly treated as illustrative content.
