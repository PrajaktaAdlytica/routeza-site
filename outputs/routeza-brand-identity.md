# Routeza Brand Identity

## Brand Summary

Routeza is a Poland and EU-focused route operations SaaS platform for delivery companies, field service teams, cold-chain operators, and local fleets.

Brand personality:
- Operational
- Calm
- Precise
- Credible
- Modern

Positioning line:
`Route planning that keeps moving after the day changes.`

## Typography Direction

Recommended display direction:
- Modern neo-grotesk or strong sans serif
- Heavy weight for headlines
- Tight line-height

Recommended UI/body direction:
- Clean sans serif
- Medium contrast
- High legibility for operational dashboards and forms

Current web implementation direction:
- Strong bold headline style
- Neutral SaaS body typography

## Visual Language

Core principles:
- Calm over flashy
- Operational evidence over decoration
- Light premium SaaS aesthetic
- Subtle motion, not demo-heavy motion
- Clean grids, strong spacing, and structured information hierarchy

Use visual motifs like:
- Route paths
- Lifecycle rails
- Operational cards
- Dispatch signals

Avoid:
- Over-animated hero demos
- Generic startup gradients
- Dark, noisy dashboard theater
- Playful or consumer-only aesthetics

## Brand Usage Notes

Use the primary logo on light backgrounds.

Use the logo mark when space is limited.

Keep generous clear space around the logo:
- Minimum clear space should be about the height of the logo dot

Do not:
- Change logo proportions
- Recolor the mark arbitrarily
- Add drop shadows directly to the logo
- Place it on low-contrast backgrounds
