# Roviaza Brand Identity

## Brand Summary

Roviaza is a Poland and EU-focused route operations SaaS platform for delivery companies, field service teams, cold-chain operators, and local fleets.

Core problem:
- Route plans break when traffic, cancellations, service windows, driver capacity, and cold-chain constraints change during the day.

Core product line:
- Roviaza Plan
- Roviaza Live
- Roviaza Replan

Brand personality:
- Precise
- Operational
- Calm
- Adaptive
- Credible

Positioning line:
`Route planning that keeps moving after the day changes.`

## Logo Meaning

The Roviaza mark is a route loop with a highlighted shortcut through it.

What it communicates:
- The muted circular route represents the full operational network
- The lime shortcut shows route intelligence finding a better path through changing conditions
- The mint center node acts as the dispatch decision point where the route is recalculated
- The outer white checkpoints keep the mark grounded in stops, handoffs, and route states

This makes the mark specifically about:
- route planning
- live adjustment
- replanning around constraints

It avoids both a generic monogram and a generic map pin, so the symbol can feel more ownable as the company grows.

## Typography Direction

Recommended display direction:
- modern neo-grotesk sans
- bold, decisive headlines
- compact line-height

Recommended UI/body direction:
- clean operational sans serif
- medium contrast
- high legibility for dashboards, tables, and forms

## Visual Language

Core principles:
- operational clarity over decoration
- premium SaaS polish
- subtle motion
- strong white-space discipline
- route intelligence as a visual motif

Use visual motifs like:
- route loops
- connected checkpoints
- controlled exception states
- evidence panels
- logistics timeline flows

Avoid:
- generic pins
- overly abstract startup symbols
- playful consumer shapes
- high-noise motion-heavy hero treatments

## Usage Notes

Use the primary logo on light surfaces.

Use the logo mark when space is limited.

Minimum clear space:
- at least the height of one node circle around the mark

Do not:
- distort the route stroke proportions
- recolor the lime shortcut arbitrarily
- remove the center decision node from the mark
- place the logo over low-contrast photography
